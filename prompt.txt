Write one line, at most 120 characters: the funniest weather forecast for
a city that does not exist.

The line must read as a weather forecast for an invented city, be funny on
a first read with the laugh coming from the weather itself (not a pun
bolted on), sound like a flat official weather bulletin that then breaks,
and make the invented city feel like a real place — a name and a detail
that fit its absurd weather. No wasted word.

The program (main.py, standard library only) writes the line to
$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt and verifies it by reading it back.
